=== Slide 7 ===
Special Rank Selection handling
In case of Resource Allocation taking place for traffic on 5QI=1, rank selected =1, irrespective of RI value reported by UE.
In case DL CQI reported by UE is below a certain threshold (LowCqiThreshold), rank selected =1. The threshold value is a configurable parameter. 
In case of DL CQI miss (PUCCH DTX) for consecutive number of times times (configurable, same Aperiodic CSI trigger count), rank shall be selected as 1, irrespective of the last rank reported by UE.
