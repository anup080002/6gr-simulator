## Rank and MCS policy scope

The original resolved YAML declares `rank_adaptation_policy: measured_ri`, `rank_increase_confirmation_reports: 2`, and MCS transition up1/down2/ACK2. Once confirmed, RI4 and its TPMI4 apply atomically. The configuration does not mandate a rank1→2→3→4 staircase. Inserting intermediate ranks while retaining rank4's TPMI would change the spatial decision without receiver support. Rank confirmation, candidate scoring and ACK counts are implementation policies; this audit does not assert they are NR requirements or coded-goodput-optimal.

`estimateSRSRITPMI.localEstimateRankTPMI` enumerates allowed ranks in ascending order and each rank's supported TPMI catalog, evaluates measured-channel MMSE per-layer signal/interference/noise powers, and maximizes average `sum(log2(1+SINR_layer))`. Its strict `metric > bestMetric` comparison keeps the first enumerated candidate on an exact tie; it does not force the maximum rank on ties. This spectral-efficiency heuristic is not a decoded-throughput or BLER optimum. No alteration of that policy is justified by the single successful UL55 trial.
