# RAN1 10.5.3.1 DL-CSI result interpretation

The `smoke` mode is an execution and artifact-integrity qualification. It is
not statistically converged and is not a common-EVM result. Analytical
equation checks may support explicitly analytical text; procedure rows may
support lifecycle descriptions only. `LLS_CONTROLLED` rows demonstrate that
the production waveform path executed under the resolved controlled profile,
but they cannot support a 3GPP quantitative gain until the exact agreed EVM,
matched baseline/candidate campaign, convergence gates and full artifact
audit all pass.

The proposal-evidence matrix and limitations table are the claim gate. A
missing waveform scenario stays `NOT_EVALUATED`; it is not replaced by
Shannon capacity, a hand-written threshold, a synthetic BLER row, or a
procedure result.
